Date Diff

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts days and works out dates on the Gregorian calendar from the dates you type, between 1900 and 2100. It does not know time zones, holidays, business days, or what any legal or contract rule counts as a day, month or year. Check those against the source that applies.

The gap truth

Days between two dates, and the same gap in whole years, months and days. Write dates as YYYY-MM-DD.

The add truth

A date a span after or before another date, with weekdays.

The birthday truth

Age on a date, and the next birthday.
